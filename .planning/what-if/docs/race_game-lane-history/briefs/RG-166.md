## Scope
1. **Mapping variants** (derive, then table): (A) local tangent plane at the quad centre + radial projection of each planar vertex onto the sphere (what RG-165 does); (B) WorldGen grid laid directly in the face's equal-angle (a, b) parameters of the quad (bilinear over the 4 corners); (C) any better published option (e.g. per-quad conformal/affine fit, geodesic grid) if it is cheap. For each: formula, what is exact, what bends, cost per vertex.
2. **Distortion budget** at three sites (face centre, cube-edge midpoint, cube vertex) for one level-15 quad: side lengths, max angle deviation from 90°, area ratio vs 256 x 256, max metric stretch (singular values of the Jacobian planar->sphere), and the **sagitta / height drop** across a 256 m tile and at 600 m (horizon) — numbers M from a short script (Python stdlib, `artifacts/tmp/rg166/`, a few seconds; source in the outbox).
3. **Roads and buildings under the deformation:** which WorldGen rules break first (curve radius, grade, lane width, straight segments, right angles of building footprints, flat foundations) and the max error per variant; whether roads should be generated in the tangent plane and only the terrain warped, or vice versa.
4. **Colliders and LOD seams:** neighbouring sectors have rotated tangent frames — how collider meshes and near/far LOD edges stay watertight (shared canonical edge vertices, skirts, taper), T-junctions between a 65 x 65 near tile and the coarser CDLOD far layer; float precision of vertices relative to the floating origin (error at 256 m / 600 m / 10 km).
5. **Horizon and visual curvature:** at what distance curvature becomes visible (drop vs camera height 1.2 m / 50 m), whether the near ring can stay planar per sector and curvature only enter through the far layer.
6. **Recommendation** (mandatory, one choice, no open A/B for the user — the user's rule since 2026-11-12): which mapping v1/v2 should use, the taper width, budget numbers the adapter must meet, and **what the next experiment must measure** (rows the orchestrator can turn into an adapter v2 ticket).
