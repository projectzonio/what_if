## Design (simplest thing)
1. In `Analyse` (r2 path, after `RoadArc.Route`): for each arc, the distance along the true arc from `a` to the first **shifted** portal (a portal whose position differs from its raw crossing by > 1 cm) and from the last shifted portal to `b`. An arc with no shifted portal is eligible. Eligible = both distances >= 1000 m.
2. r2 protocol row "road length change, max %, < 0.5" over **eligible arcs only**; the row text states eligible / total arcs.
3. Diag rows (report, not thresholded): excluded arcs count and share of all 3000; max length change among excluded arcs; the old all-arcs max (0.5236 % expected) so the change of metric stays visible.
4. Shift rows: protocol "single-portal snap shift (candidate B, `Portal.Snap`), max m, <= 256"; diag "candidate A: max lateral offset of the shifted road line from the true arc, m" and "candidate A: max slide of a crossing along its border, m" (7100.6 m expected).
