# RG-180 — Sweep: schod na okraji silnice — kolmý nájezd z trávy na silnici

Read AGENTS.md, the pool README, `.planning/race-game/tasks/RG-180.md`, `wiki/concept` pages on the rain + flood test (RG-122) and the skills `rg-unity-notebook`, `rg-agent-port`.

## Why
User's ride RG-053 (NSX, seed 1, 2026-11-12), verbatim: „Některé silnice jsou ok, některé mají bug- při nájezdu z trávy/okolí na silnici kolmo- náraz. Při nájezdu šikmo na silnici ok, jako přejet obrubník. Když modifikujeme záznam trasy na označování bodů, tak můžu flagnout několik silnic a snadno najít důvod. Jak upravit flood test, aby pokryl kolize směrem k silnici z různých úhlů?“

Orchestrator's reading of the flood pass (`engine-unity/NightRacer/Assets/Scripts/Streaming/WorldGenSweepFlood.cs:8-20`): it walks **from the road outward**; a move may go down by up to 1.0 m ("a car can drop off a kerb-sized ledge") but up only 0.3 m, and `trap` counts only cells with no way back at all. `step` checks only consecutive lane-centre hits. So a 10-30 cm step at the road edge, which a wheel climbs at an angle but hits head-on, is never reported. Road y = H(s) + Lift, shoulder y = H(s) + 0.02 (`WorldGenSweepRoads.cs:12`): the difference between the road outline and the terrain just outside it is the first suspect, not yet measured.

## Scope
1. **Geometry defect `edge_step`** in the sweep: at every station (2 m) on both road outlines of the centre sector, ray down at 0.3 m inside and at 0.5 / 1.0 / 2.0 m outside the outline; report the height step (road edge minus ground) and any non-road face with `normal.y < 0.7` in that band. Threshold: step > 0.08 m (state why if you choose another). Histogram per road class and per edge kind (ground road, bridge approach, junction, city street).
2. **Physical confirmation:** on the N = 20 worst stations, place the car (arcade-u2) on the verge 8 m out, heading 90 / 60 / 30 deg to the road, 30 and 50 km/h; record speed loss across the edge and the peak vertical contact impulse. Agent port or a sweep sub-mode, your choice.
3. **Cause + fix** of the geometry (road collider edge, shoulder, terrain carve) so that head-on approach behaves like the oblique one.
4. Optional, only if cheap: an F5 mark in `--stream-run manual` writes the pose to the ride report (the user offered to flag roads in a ride).

## Acceptance
- Sweep seed 1 and one more WorldGen seed: `edge_step` count and histogram before / after; after the fix 0 above the threshold, or every remaining case named with its reason.
- Physical table (worst stations x 3 angles x 2 speeds): speed loss and vertical impulse before / after.
- Ride queued with `manual-test.py new RG-180` (spawn at the worst station found, head-on approach from the grass; questions: impact head-on? at an angle? anything else).
- Golden hashes: state which change (terrain golden may change only if the cause is in `Terrain.cs`; then report old/new hash and why). `scripts/check.ps1` and `scripts/verify-hygiene.ps1` exit 0.
