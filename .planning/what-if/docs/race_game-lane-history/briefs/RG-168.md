## Design (simplest thing that answers the questions; you may improve it, say why in the outbox)
New library `src/WhatIf.Planet.Surface` (netstandard2.1, C# 8, references only `WhatIf.Planet`, no Unity, no packages). Everything is a pure function of `(worldSeed, dir)` evaluated in double with the existing int64 lattice noise (`PlanetNoise.Value/Fbm`, or your own cached sampler with the same arithmetic). **Nothing is stored**; seams across faces/regions/sectors are solved by construction because there is no face parameterisation anywhere (3D lattice on the planet-fixed position).

1. **`PlanetSurface`** (seed) with `Height(Vec3 dir)` (metres above the sphere R, sea level = 0) and `Sample(Vec3 dir) -> SurfaceSample { double HeightM; SurfaceKind Kind; double WaterLevelM; float Forest; byte Biome; }`. `Height(dir)` must return exactly the bits of `Sample(dir).HeightM` (RG-169 passes `Height` to `Generator.Run` as the base).
2. **Continents + ocean:** continent field `c(dir)` = domain-warped fBm at planet scale (suggestion: cells 2^21 .. 2^17 m, 5 octaves, warp cell 2^20); map `c` through one smooth monotone curve to elevation: abyssal floor ~-4000 m, shelf -200..0 m, lowland 0..~300 m, uplands/mountains to ~+2500 m (ridged term only above the uplands). Add the existing 150 m detail (`PlanetNoise.Height`) on land **scaled by a smooth factor of the continent elevation** so that the coastline is drawn by the continent field, not by detail speckle. Kinds: `Ocean` (h < -200 m), `Sea` (-200 <= h < 0, shelf), `Beach` (0 <= h < 2 m on the coast), `Land`, `Lake` (below a lake level, item 3). Sea level is exactly 0.
3. **Lakes = cells, not simulation** (no flood fill; that would need neighbours and break the pure function): a 3D int64 cell lattice of 2^13 m (8192 m). A cell may hold one lake (hash probability p, your tuning): centre = normalize(cell centre + jitter within +-0.25 cell) * R, radius 250-1200 m with a low-frequency angular wobble in the centre's tangent plane. **The lake exists only if its whole disc (max radius + bank band) lies inside its own 3D cell** and every rim sample is >= 2 m above sea level -> a sample needs only its own cell = seamless and cheap. Level `L` = minimum of the lake-free height over N rim samples (e.g. 64) minus 0.5 m, cached per cell (bounded LRU, not thread safe, like `HeightSampler`). Inside: lake bed below L (depth 3-12 m, smooth profile from the shore); outside in a bank band (~40 m): height blended so the shoreline is >= L and joins the natural terrain continuously. Rows s4/s6 measure it.
4. **Forest + biome:** temperature from latitude (pole axis = the planet's +Y, document it) minus 6.5 K/km of elevation; moisture = fBm at 2^17 .. 2^14 m; a small biome table (e.g. ice, tundra, boreal, temperate, grassland, desert) -> `Forest` density in [0, 1] with smooth transitions (continuous inputs -> continuous density). No forest on `Ocean/Sea/Lake/Beach`, above the tree line, or on slopes > 30° (if you compute slope, do it from the same function, finite difference in the tangent plane). The near layer (WorldGen `Scatter`) keeps its own mask; RG-169 decides how they combine.
5. **Sector sampler:** `SurfaceSector.Sample(SectorId, ...)` fills 65 x 65 arrays (height, kind, forest) at `QuadSector.Direction(i, j)` in one batch with a per-octave corner cache and the lake-cell cache. This is the hot path the budget row measures.
6. **Experiment console** `src/WhatIf.Planet.Surface.Experiment` + runner `tools/whatif-surface/run-surface.ps1` (copy the pattern of `tools/whatif-planet/run-experiment.ps1`: refuse while a `NightRacer*` player runs, take the measurement lock via `tools/game-unity/measure-lock.ps1` — RG-167's runner takes the same lock, so the two never measure at once — Release build, cost rows in 3 separate processes, raw output `artifacts/whatif-surface/<stamp>/` git-ignored, sha256 list). Report generator writes `.planning/race-game/docs/what-if-planet-surface.{md,csv}` (same CSV columns/kinds as `what-if-planet-experiment.csv`: row, kind protocol|report, value, threshold, result, note).
7. **Images (for agents, not for the user)** into the raw folder as PNG (write them with `System.IO.Compression.ZLibStream`, ~40 lines, no package): (i) equirectangular whole planet 2048 x 1024, colour by kind/biome + hillshade; (ii) the 6 cube faces 512 x 512; (iii) three level-10 regions at 16 m (~612 x 612): one on a coast, one with a lake, one forested upland; (iv) one 2 x 2 km crop at 4 m across a lake shore. List them with sha256 in the doc. The orchestrator opens them at review: the planet must read as continents + oceans, the regions as coast / lake / forest.

## Acceptance (rows; 3 seeds unless said otherwise)
- **s1 seamless + deterministic (protocol):** 10 000 canonical lattice vertices on cube edges and vertices evaluated through every face that shares them, plus 10 000 random directions evaluated twice in two separate processes: differing bits of every `SurfaceSample` field = **0**. `Height(dir)` bits == `Sample(dir).HeightM` bits.
- **s2 planet shape:** 1 000 000 uniform random directions: land share (Land + Beach + Lake) **25-40 %** (protocol); report ocean / sea / beach / lake / forest shares, number of continents >= 1 000 000 km² and the largest one's share of land on the 2048 x 1024 map (report; expect >= 3 continents, largest < 70 %).
- **s3 coast quality (report):** on the three region maps: share of land in islands < 1 ha, coastline length per region, max height at the coast within 100 m of the shoreline. A speckled coast (> 5 % of land in < 1 ha islands) is a finding — fix it in this ticket (the design note in item 2).
- **s4 lakes:** lakes per 10 000 km² of land over 200 random land regions (report; aim 2-20); for every lake in those regions: shoreline walked at 1 m steps, terrain below the lake level outside the lake = **0 points** (protocol, no spill); lake surface level constant per lake (protocol, by construction); no lake within 2 m of sea level (protocol).
- **s5 forest:** forest share of land **20-55 %** (protocol); forest > 0 on Ocean/Sea/Lake/Beach or above the tree line = **0 samples** (protocol); forest density continuity: max |delta forest| between neighbour 4 m vertices <= 0.05 (protocol).
- **s6 continuity:** max |delta h| between neighbour lattice vertices (~4 m apart) in 300 sectors (100 coast, 100 lake shore, 100 random land) <= **4 m** (protocol; = 45°); report P99 and the worst sector id.
- **s7 cost (HW budget, protocol):** `SurfaceSector.Sample` 65 x 65, single thread, median of 3 processes **<= 1.5 ms** on this notebook (P95 report); `Height(dir)` ns/call report; lake-cell cache + samplers managed memory **<= 16 MiB** after 1000 sectors (report the number); whole-planet 2048 x 1024 map time report. If s7 fails, optimise in this ticket (corner cache, skip octaves below the sample spacing, early-out of the lake cell) before reporting a FAIL.
- **s8 no regression:** `run-experiment.ps1` is not touched; `scripts/check.ps1` + `scripts/verify-hygiene.ps1` exit 0 (`WhatIf.Planet.Surface.Tests` added to `scripts/test.ps1` and the sln). Unit tests: s1 bit-identity on a few hundred points, lake no-spill on a fixed seed, `Height == Sample.HeightM`.
- A failing protocol row: report FAIL with numbers and the cause; do not tune thresholds after the fact. Under the What If mandate the orchestrator decides at review.
