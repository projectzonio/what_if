## Review history and root causes
- **Review 1.** Acceptance not met: seam_gap 0/1/3 and surface_mismatch at span ends 0/2/1. The orchestrator found the cause in code: the detector cut edges on the perpendicular plane, while the generator uses a mitre since RG-119, so the wedge on the outer side of a bend belonged to no edge. It also kept a bare outline for bridges. The worker had stopped at 1.0 m, because the detector was outside their ownership.
- **Rework 1.** seam/surface/wall 0/0/0, but a new seed 1 `trap` appeared (3 cells beside edge 3).
- **Rework 2.** The barrier gap over the hold zone was removed: it was obsolete at a 1.5 m deck and was a hole at an elevated edge. The trap stayed.
  - The worker's `[trapdbg]` log showed the flood stepping down 0.77 m from deck cells straight onto the terrain.
  - Orchestrator reading: `WorldGenSweepFlood` calls `Barrier()` only for an empty neighbour (a fall). A valid neighbour lower by at most `MaxDown` (1.0 m) passes `Allowed()` with no barrier test.
  - So this is a false positive of the flood detector, not geometry; the barrier stands on the edge (worker shots `trap0/1.png`).
  - Out of this ticket's ownership, so it is a follow-up for the sweep-gate ticket.
