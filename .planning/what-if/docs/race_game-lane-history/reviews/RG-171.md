## Decisions (What If mandate: adjust with a justification, report here + handover)
1. **f5.no_gap.drive accepted.** The row's purpose is "no visible hole"; that holds (0 gap frames). The strict "parent built" condition misses in 3 of 7200 frames for horizon nodes 176-182 km away, where a built grandparent is drawn instead. A speed-tied prefetch margin goes into the Unity viewer ticket (it measures what the eye sees there), not into a rework.
2. **Range13 = 1800 m with an automatic morph fraction (2 % floor)** accepted as the worker chose (a 30 % zone needs range13 >= 3.5 km and breaks the 400-node budget). Risk: the popping zone is narrow (0.15 node sizes) — the viewer ticket must look at popping at 108 km/h and in flight.
3. Ground-point distance (`min(d_camera, d_ground)`) and shell-cap bounds accepted (exact, cheaper than boxes).
4. Carried forward: RG-169's coast change shifts the surface the far layer samples — rerun `run-farlayer.ps1` after RG-169 merges (f2 skirt depth may change; skirt = measured max x 1.25). WorldGen carve near roads adds to the near/far gap; RG-169 or the viewer ticket measures it on the hole edge.
5. Water: the far layer draws the sea floor; the water surface is the viewer's job (noted in the doc).
