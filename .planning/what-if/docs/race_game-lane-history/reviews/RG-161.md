## Is the (a) FAIL real? Yes.
Read `src/WhatIf.Planet/SectorFrame.cs`: a sector centre = radial drop of `regionCentre*R + east*(ix*256) + north*(iz*256)` in the **region's** tangent plane (gnomonic grid, axes from the region anchor). Two neighbouring regions have independent grids: phase offset up to one sector and axes rotated (~1.5e-3 rad per 10 km of anchor-east convergence; tens of degrees at a cube corner). The diagnostic rows place B exactly 256 m across A (frame effect only) and still give 184 mm (R45) / 94 m (RC). Vertical (radial) mismatch is ~2e-6 mm everywhere, as predicted. In-region horizontal 0.263 mm < 1 mm in all four regions incl. the far corner 4.6 km from the anchor.

So the ADR §4 rule "(a) fails at region borders -> take the sector frame from the region anchor" is **already what the run does** and does not close the seam: square planar 256 m grids per region cannot tile a sphere seamlessly. This is geometry, not a pairing bug. The gap is in WI-ADR-001 row 3 ("sector grid inside a region is for naming only" says nothing about how grids of neighbouring regions meet).
