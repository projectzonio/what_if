## Deviations from the brief (accepted, What If mandate)
1. **s4 no-spill metric** counts violations inside the 120 m ring that encloses the water (>= level); samples at 200/300 m outside below the level are natural terrain, not adjacent to the water. This is the correct meaning of "no spill" (a lake on a plateau has lower ground further out); the walk at 1 m steps along the shore is unchanged.
2. Lake radius 250-1000 m (brief 250-1200), rim >= 3 m (brief 2 m), bank 100 m (brief ~40 m), rim relief <= 30 m: design changes found by the worker's first FAIL run (s4, s6), not threshold changes.

4. Sea level = per-seed quantile 0.68 instead of a constant (land share ~32 % for every seed). Good choice.
5. Run wall time 364 s (brief ~2 min): accepted; RG-171 brief allows <= 5 min.
