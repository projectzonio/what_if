## Design (what the library does)

**Nodes.** The existing equal-angle quadtree per cube face, levels 0..13. A node (face, l, row, col) covers 2^(21-l) lattice steps and has 17 x 17 vertices (16 x 16 quads) at lattice spacing 2^(17-l) steps, so every vertex is a lattice vertex and goes through `Lattice.Direction` (canonical on cube edges): shared edge vertices of neighbouring nodes, also across faces, have identical double bits (row f8). Level 13 = 1.2 km nodes, ~76 m spacing, exactly 4 x 4 level-15 sectors = 4 x 4 quads each. A node stores only its 289 heights (double, 2312 B) and its bounds; positions are recomputed from the lattice (`FarNode.Position`), the viewer buffers (xyz + morph delta, 289 x 24 B) are filled at upload (`FarMesh.FillBuffers`).

**Bounds.** Shell cap: cone of half angle alpha (corner directions of the node) around the node's centre direction, radii R + hmin - 40 m .. R + hmax + 40 m. Height range = the node's own vertices once built, else the parent's (levels 0..3 are prebuilt, so it is always defined). The distance camera -> cap is exact and needs only a dot product, two square roots and no trigonometry per node (tighter than a bounding sphere or an axis-aligned box of the corner points, which would ignore the bulge of the surface).

**Selection (CDLOD).** Recursive from the six face roots. A node of level l splits when `min(d_camera, d_ground) < LodRange(l)` with `LodRange(l) = Range13 * 2^(13-l)` (Range13 = 1950 m), or when it contains a near sector. `d_ground` is the distance of the ground point under the camera (the centre of the near set) to the cap: this is the "distance to the near set" of the brief; it grades the quadtree around the hole, so near the hole level 13 reaches ~3.6 km around the ground point also when the camera flies 10 km above it, and the neighbour-level rule holds there (a plain camera distance would force level 13 next to level 10 at altitude). With LodRange(l) larger than twice the node diagonal any two neighbouring leaves differ by at most one level (measured by row f8). **Horizon culling**: a node is dropped when its angular distance to the camera direction is larger than the horizon angle of the camera over the sea-level sphere plus the horizon angle of the node's highest point (hmax + 40 m, sea level counts as 0); a camera below the sea-level sphere culls nothing. Nodes up to 0.01 rad (64 km) beyond that limit are only *requested* (built ahead). Optional 90 x 59 degree frustum (planes against the node's bounding sphere), used for the "frustum" numbers only.

**Morph.** Per vertex, `m = clamp((d - start) / (end - start))` with d = min(distance to camera, distance to ground point) of the unmorphed vertex, end = LodRange(l-1), start = end * (1 - f). Odd vertices move to the midpoint of their even grid neighbours (odd-odd: the diagonal (i-1, j-1)-(i+1, j+1); every quad is split on the diagonal (a, b)-(a+1, b+1), so the centre vertex lies exactly on the parent's diagonal). `FarMesh` is the CPU reference of the vertex shader; row f7 checks at m = 1 against an independently built parent node. **Morph fraction f is automatic** (0.02 .. 0.3): a fine edge vertex next to a coarser leaf lies at distance up to LodRange(l) + node diagonal from the camera, and the coarse node's own morph zone must not have started there, else its odd edge vertices have already moved off the fine node's even vertices and a crack of metres opens (measured: f = 0.3 gave gaps of 20 m, f = 0.15 with Range13 2000 still 8 m). The bound is f <= 0.5 - 0.725 * side / LodRange(l); at Range13 1800 m this is the 2 % floor, i.e. the morph zone is 4 % of the range wide (0.15 node sizes) - the price of keeping the node count below 400.

**Hole mask.** `NearSet` = level-15 sectors whose centre is within 600 m of the ground point (BFS over `SectorId.Neighbour`, so it crosses cube edges). A level-13 node gets a 16-bit mask (bit (row & 3) * 4 + (col & 3) of its sectors); the 4 x 4 quads of a masked sector are not drawn. All ancestors of a near sector are forced to split, so a mask only ever exists on level 13. **Skirt**: on every quad edge next to a dropped quad a vertical strip of depth skirtM = max measured |near - far| x 1.25 rounded up to 0.5 m (row f2); the skirt triangles = 2 x 4 quads per exposed sector side are counted in f3 (`NearSet.Exposed`).

**Cache and builds.** `FarLayer.Node` creates the node record (bounds only) on first visit; `Build` = 289 `Height` calls. LRU bounded at 12 000 records (levels 0..3 pinned, nothing visited in the current frame is evicted). `Select` returns the nodes to draw and the build requests; a leaf that is not built is drawn by its nearest built ancestor (children are never mixed with their parent: the whole group falls back), and its chain is requested coarse levels first, then nearest first; `BuildPending(budgetMs)` builds until the 2.0 ms per frame budget is spent. A fallback ancestor that contains near sectors is refused (it cannot carry a mask), counted as a gap (`IncompleteRoots`).

**Decisions for the Unity viewer.** Upload one vertex buffer per built node (xyz relative to `FarNode.Origin` + morph delta), one shared index buffer for the 16 x 16 grid with the fixed diagonal, and per draw the 16-bit hole mask (skip the masked 4 x 4 quad blocks, e.g. by 16 index sub-ranges or a discard in the vertex stage), the node origin and level for the morph. The morph factor is computed in the vertex shader from the distance of the vertex to the camera **and** to the ground point (the near set centre), with the range of its level; both ranges come from `FarParams`. Skirt: one strip per exposed sector side, bottom = top - skirtM radially. Water is not part of this layer (the sea floor is drawn; the water surface is the viewer's).
